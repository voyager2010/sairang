Munnar

Discover the breathtaking beauty of Munnar, a picturesque hill station in Kerala known for its lush tea plantations, misty mountains, waterfalls, wildlife, and peaceful atmosphere.

About Munnar

Munnar is one of Kerala's most beautiful hill stations, located in the Western Ghats. Famous for its endless tea gardens, cool climate, mist-covered hills, waterfalls, and wildlife sanctuaries, Munnar is a perfect destination for nature lovers, honeymoon couples, photographers, and adventure enthusiasts. The peaceful surroundings and scenic beauty make it one of India's top tourist destinations.

Top Places to Visit

Tea Gardens

Endless green tea plantations offering breathtaking views and refreshing walking trails.

Eravikulam National Park

Home to the endangered Nilgiri Tahr and stunning mountain landscapes.

Mattupetty Dam

A peaceful lake surrounded by hills where visitors can enjoy boating and photography.

Attukad Waterfalls

One of Munnar's most beautiful waterfalls, especially during the monsoon season.

How to Reach Munnar

By Air

Nearest Airport:
Cochin International Airport (110 km)

Taxis and buses are available to Munnar.

By Train

Nearest Station:
Aluva Railway Station (110 km)

Well connected with major Indian cities.

By Bus

Regular KSRTC and private buses operate from Kochi, Madurai, Coimbatore, and Thekkady.

By Road

Munnar is connected by scenic hill roads from Kochi, Thekkady, Coimbatore, and Madurai.
