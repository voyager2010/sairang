Jaipur

Explore Jaipur, the Pink City of India, famous for its magnificent forts, royal palaces, colorful bazaars, rich Rajasthani culture, and delicious cuisine.

About Jaipur

Jaipur, the capital of Rajasthan, is popularly known as the "Pink City" because of its beautiful pink-colored buildings. Founded in 1727 by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II, Jaipur is famous for its royal palaces, magnificent forts, vibrant markets, traditional handicrafts, and rich cultural heritage. It is one of India's most popular tourist destinations and forms part of the famous Golden Triangle.

Top Places to Visit

Hawa Mahal

The iconic Palace of Winds with 953 beautiful windows, offering a glimpse into Jaipur's royal history.

Amber Fort

A majestic hilltop fort known for its grand architecture, Sheesh Mahal, and panoramic city views.

City Palace

A magnificent royal palace featuring museums, courtyards, and stunning Rajput architecture.

Jal Mahal

A beautiful palace situated in the middle of Man Sagar Lake, famous for its scenic beauty.

Famous Food in Jaipur

Rajasthan's signature dish made with baked baati, spicy dal, and sweet churma.

Ghewar

A traditional Rajasthani sweet made with flour, sugar syrup, and dry fruits.

A famous spicy Rajasthani mutton curry cooked with red chilies and aromatic spices.

How to Reach Jaipur

By Air

Nearest Airport:
Jaipur International Airport (JAI)

Connected to all major Indian cities.

By Train

Jaipur Junction Railway Station is well connected with Delhi, Mumbai, Ahmedabad, and other cities.

By Bus

Regular Rajasthan Roadways, Volvo, and private buses operate from Delhi, Agra, Udaipur, and Jodhpur.

By Road

Jaipur is connected by NH48 and excellent highways, making road travel comfortable from nearby cities.
