Kashmir

Discover the breathtaking beauty of Kashmir, where snow-covered mountains, serene lakes, colorful gardens, and lush valleys create an unforgettable travel experience.

About Kashmir

Kashmir, often called the "Paradise on Earth", is one of India's most breathtaking travel destinations. Surrounded by snow-capped Himalayan mountains, crystal-clear lakes, lush green valleys, colorful gardens, and charming houseboats, Kashmir offers a perfect blend of natural beauty and adventure. Whether you're seeking peaceful landscapes, thrilling activities, or rich local culture, Kashmir promises an unforgettable experience for every traveler.

Top Places to Visit

Dal Lake

Famous for its peaceful Shikara rides, floating markets, and traditional houseboats with breathtaking mountain views.

Gulmarg

A paradise for skiing, snowboarding, cable car rides, and enjoying snow-covered Himalayan landscapes.

Pahalgam

A beautiful hill station surrounded by pine forests, rivers, valleys, and scenic trekking routes.

Sonamarg

Known as the "Meadow of Gold", Sonamarg offers glaciers, snow peaks, camping, and unforgettable mountain views.

Famous Food in Kashmir

How to Reach Kashmir

By Air

Nearest Airport:
Srinagar International Airport (SXR)

Connected with Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Jammu, and other major cities.

By Train

Nearest Station:
Jammu Tawi Railway Station (190 km)

Taxis and buses are available to Srinagar.

By Bus

Regular buses operate from Jammu to Srinagar through NH44. Deluxe and Volvo buses are also available.

By Road

Kashmir is connected via National Highway 44. Travel by private car, taxi, or bike to enjoy scenic mountain views.
