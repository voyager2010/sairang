Hampi, Karnataka

Discover the ancient ruins, magnificent temples, and rich history of the UNESCO World Heritage Site.

About Hampi

Hampi is a UNESCO World Heritage Site located in Karnataka, India. It was once the capital of the Vijayanagara Empire and is famous for its magnificent temples, giant boulders, ancient monuments, and rich history. Every year, thousands of tourists visit Hampi to experience its unique architecture and breathtaking landscapes.

Top Places to Visit

Virupaksha Temple

One of the oldest temples in Hampi dedicated to Lord Shiva.

Stone Chariot

The iconic stone chariot located inside Vittala Temple.

Lotus Mahal

A beautiful palace with unique Indo-Islamic architecture.

Matanga Hill

The best place to enjoy sunrise and sunset in Hampi.
