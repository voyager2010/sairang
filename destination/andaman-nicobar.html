Andaman & Nicobar Islands

Discover the tropical paradise of the Andaman & Nicobar Islands, known for crystal-clear waters, white sandy beaches, coral reefs, water sports, and breathtaking island scenery.

About Andaman & Nicobar Islands

The Andaman & Nicobar Islands are among India's most beautiful tropical destinations, located in the Bay of Bengal. Famous for turquoise waters, white-sand beaches, coral reefs, marine life, and adventure sports, these islands are perfect for honeymooners, scuba divers, nature lovers, and families looking for a peaceful getaway.

Top Places to Visit

Radhanagar Beach

One of Asia's most beautiful beaches, famous for its white sand and spectacular sunsets.

Havelock Island

A paradise for scuba diving, snorkeling, kayaking, and beach lovers.

Cellular Jail

A historic national memorial known for India's freedom struggle and evening light show.

Neil Island

A peaceful island with coral reefs, natural rock formations, and crystal-clear waters.

How to Reach Andaman & Nicobar Islands

By Air

Nearest Airport:
Veer Savarkar International Airport, Port Blair.

Direct flights from Chennai, Kolkata, Bengaluru, Delhi, and Hyderabad.

By Ship

Passenger ships operate from Chennai, Kolkata, and Visakhapatnam to Port Blair.

Local Transport

Government buses, taxis, rental scooters, and ferries connect major islands.

By Ferry

Regular ferries connect Port Blair, Havelock Island, Neil Island, and nearby islands.
