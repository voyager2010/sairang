Gateway of India

Visit the iconic Gateway of India in Mumbai, a magnificent monument overlooking the Arabian Sea. Experience its rich history, beautiful architecture, boat rides, and the vibrant atmosphere of India's financial capital.

About Gateway of India

The Gateway of India is one of Mumbai's most famous landmarks, located on the waterfront overlooking the Arabian Sea. Built in 1924 to commemorate the visit of King George V and Queen Mary, this magnificent Indo-Saracenic monument attracts millions of visitors every year. It is also the starting point for ferry rides to Elephanta Caves and offers breathtaking views of the sea.

How to Reach Gateway of India

By Air

Nearest Airport:
Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (26 km)

By Train

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus and Churchgate Railway Station are the nearest railway stations.

By Bus

BEST buses, taxis, and auto-rickshaws provide easy access from all parts of Mumbai.

By Road

Easily accessible by private car, taxi, or cab services via South Mumbai.
