Taj Mahal

Discover the timeless beauty of the Taj Mahal, one of the Seven Wonders of the World. Explore magnificent Mughal architecture, beautiful gardens, and the symbol of eternal love in Agra.

About Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal, located in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, is one of the world's most famous monuments and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Built by Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his beloved wife Mumtaz Mahal, this white marble masterpiece attracts millions of visitors every year with its breathtaking beauty and fascinating history.

Top Places to Visit

Taj Mahal

One of the Seven Wonders of the World, famous for its white marble architecture.

Agra Fort

Historic Mughal fort built with red sandstone and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Itimad-ud-Daulah

Popularly known as the Baby Taj because of its beautiful marble architecture.

Mehtab Bagh

A beautiful riverside garden offering the best sunset view of the Taj Mahal.

Famous Food in Agra

How to Reach Taj Mahal

By Air

Nearest Airport:
Agra Airport (AGR)

Delhi Airport is about 230 km away.

By Train

Agra Cantt Railway Station is well connected with Delhi, Mumbai, Jaipur and other cities.

By Bus

Frequent buses operate from Delhi, Jaipur, Lucknow and nearby cities.

By Road

Easily accessible via the Yamuna Expressway and NH19 from Delhi and surrounding cities.
